What Archipelago is

Archipelago is a learning tool: you chart what you understand about a subject as islands on a map, and AI personas (a cartographer, a Mentor and a Keeper) sketch, explain and ask you questions. It is run by an independent developer based in Taiwan (“we”).

The software is open source under the MIT License (github.com/funclosure/archipelago). That license covers the code. These terms cover the service we host.

Who can use it

You need to be at least 13. If you are under 18, use it with a parent or guardian's permission, and only they may buy credit.

Your charts and your words

What you write and chart is yours. It is stored in your browser, not on our servers, so keeping a copy is up to you: download a backup from the domain menu.

To answer you, your messages (with the images you attach) and the chart around them are sent to the AI model you are using. archipelago.school/privacy says exactly what goes where.

AI answers

The personas are AI models. They can be wrong, out of date, or confidently mistaken. Check anything that matters, and do not treat a reply as medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

The map's marks (lit landmarks, settled or mountainous islands) are a study aid. They are not a grade, a certificate or a qualification.
